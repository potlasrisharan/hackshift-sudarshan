# Judge questions and honest answers

**Why not just ChatGPT?** The four deterministic engines expose inputs, constraints,
intermediate probabilities and rejection reasons. A language model does not choose
medicine stock, override clinical safety screens or invent the source record.

**Why combine these projects?** Intake produces a prescription, operations knows
which stock can complete that course, and home follow-up can close the next handoff.
Our synthetic journey demonstrates those contracts; live connectors remain pending.

**Is this a medical device?** This build is a synthetic decision-support demonstration.
Real intended use would require professional regulatory classification advice and
clinical validation. The label alone does not determine regulatory status.

**How is data private?** Public demo data is synthetic and session-isolated. The
separate local Cognee proof uses local generation, local embeddings and stores,
with telemetry off and a Python socket guard. We do not claim encrypted durable
clinical records, end-to-end mobile sync or a system-wide zero-egress audit yet.

**How do you stop harmful natural-remedy advice?** The current product does not
generate herbs, prescribe formulations or replace medication. General wellbeing
content has explicit limitations. The original project's unsafe diagnosis fallback
is not called by the unified demo. A real feature needs interaction rules and
clinician review before it is enabled.

**Are the benchmark numbers real?** They are generated by the committed seed and
code, on constructed synthetic cases. They demonstrate mechanisms, not clinical
accuracy, outcome improvements or superiority over real pharmacy systems.

**What is live?** Local web/API, tested synthetic record/algorithms and standalone
local Cognee recall. Mobile is typechecked with build status recorded. No public AWS
URL, live clinical backend, ABDM linkage or wearable connection is claimed.

**What remains?** Read docs/PROGRESS.md. Provider/account access is needed for live
verification, and substantial production identity, events, native sync and clinical
module work remains explicitly pending.
